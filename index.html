03 / Research

Selected publications.

ICML 2026

AgentSuite: Toward More Reliable Agent Evaluation with a Component-Based Benchmark Auditing Pipeline

H. Suh*, S. Lee*, B. Ji*, R. Khare, B. Khan, H. Kim, Tianyi Zhang, et al.

A component-based pipeline that audits validity flaws in LLM-agent benchmarks, matches expert judgments across six benchmarks, and materially changes model rankings.

AIME 2025 · First author

Causal Brain Connectivity: Integrating Granger Directed Graphs in fMRI Analysis

Tianyi Zhang, K. Han, J. Nie, et al.

Recovers directional brain-connectivity dependencies invisible to correlation graphs and improves diagnostic prediction across roughly 8,000 patients.

Models temporal transitions across latent functional-connectivity states and outperforms static-connectivity baselines on clinical prediction.
