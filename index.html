01About

I’m a master’s student in computer science at Georgia Tech, graduating in May 2027.

Most recently I worked on LLM-based retrieval at LinkedIn. Before that, at GT EIC Lab × NVIDIA AI Research, I helped build tools for evaluating agents and auditing benchmarks. My research began at Emory, with directed and time-varying brain networks.

My interests are representation learning, LLM post-training, and model and agent evaluation. I’m particularly interested in how models learn from sparse or noisy feedback.
