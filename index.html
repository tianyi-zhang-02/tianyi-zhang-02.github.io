About

I am an M.S. student at Georgia Tech and an ML Engineer Intern with LinkedIn Topicality AI. Previously, I studied Quantitative Sciences at Emory University.

At Emory, I worked on graph neural networks for directed and temporal brain-network modeling. I later joined ×GT EIC × NVIDIA AI Research, building evaluation and benchmark-auditing systems for AI agents.

I am now interested in how models form useful representations of people and content, search over information, and improve through post-training and evaluation from sparse human feedback.
